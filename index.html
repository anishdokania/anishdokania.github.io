About

I graduated from the University of Houston in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Mathematics. Along the way I interned in data science at Allstate, built generative-AI tooling at Ambit Software, spent two years processing flight data for NASA-funded balloon missions, and won two hackathons.

Most of my own work is quantitative trading research. I turn discretionary options playbooks into testable rules, then build what it takes to test them properly: data capture, point-in-time backtesters, risk rules and trade journals. I would rather call a result noise than call it edge too early.

I am now studying sports analytics as a postgraduate at Barça Innovation Hub, and I keep building with large language models: structured outputs, LLM-as-judge checks and guardrails that keep a human in the loop.
